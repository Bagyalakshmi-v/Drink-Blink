# Drink & Blink 💧👀

*Stay hydrated. Rest your eyes. Keep coding.*

## Features

💧 **Water reminders.** Every 45 minutes, an animated character waves, holds up a glass of water and tells you to drink. Click **I drank water ✅** to count it toward your daily goal of 8 glasses, or **Snooze 😴** for 10 minutes.

👀 **Blinking eyes.** A pair of animated eyes in the bottom panel (the **Drink & Blink** tab) blinks and looks around to remind you to blink. Every 20 minutes it calls an eye break: look at something far away for 20 seconds (the 20-20-20 rule).

📊 **Status bar.** `👁 💧 3/8 · 12m` shows how many glasses you've had today and the minutes until the next reminder. Click 💧 for a reminder now; click 👁 to open the eyes panel.

## Commands

- **Drink & Blink: Remind Me Now**
- **Drink & Blink: Pause / Resume**
- **Drink & Blink: Reset Today's Count**
- **Drink & Blink: Eye-Care Tip**

## Settings

Search **Drink & Blink** in Settings:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `drinkBlink.intervalMinutes` | 45 | Minutes between water reminders |
| `drinkBlink.snoozeMinutes` | 10 | Minutes to wait when you press Snooze |
| `drinkBlink.dailyGoal` | 8 | Glasses of water per day |
| `drinkBlink.messages` | 5 messages | What the character says (one picked at random) |
| `drinkBlink.blinkEye` | true | Show the blinking eye in the status bar |
| `drinkBlink.blinkEverySeconds` | 4 | How often the status-bar eye blinks |
